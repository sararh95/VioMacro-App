Spicy Garlic Salmon

public allrecipes.com • verified 98% Confidence

A quick and flavorful salmon dish with a spicy kick from chili powder and cayenne pepper, balanced with garlic and herbs.

Prep 10 min
Cook 15 min
Servings 4

Instructions

1

Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Grease a baking sheet with cooking spray.

2

Mix brown sugar, chili powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper together in a small bowl.

3

Place salmon fillets skin-side down on the prepared baking sheet. Rub spice mixture over salmon fillets.
