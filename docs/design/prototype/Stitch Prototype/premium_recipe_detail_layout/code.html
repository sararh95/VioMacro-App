Zesty Mediterranean Salmon

A metabolism-boosting, nutrient-dense recipe designed for sustained energy. Rich in Omega-3s and high-quality proteins.

Macro Snapshot

420 Calories
38g Protein
12g Carbs
22g Fats
Prep Time 15 Mins
Difficulty Easy
Adjust Servings
2
psychology Mindful Eating Tip

"Try to chew each bite 15-20 times. This helps your brain register satiety signals, perfectly complementing this meal's high-protein content for metabolic balance."

Ingredients

2 Servings

Preparation

1

Prepare the Salmon

Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt, pepper, and a dash of lemon zest.

2

Sear to Perfection

Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Place salmon skin-side down and cook for 4-5 minutes until skin is crispy.

3

Add Aromatics

Flip the salmon. Add halved tomatoes and garlic to the pan. Cook for another 3 minutes until tomatoes are slightly blistered and salmon is cooked through.
