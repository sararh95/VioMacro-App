Today, in one glance.
Honest when there is nothing to say.

This is the screen people open most, and it is currently the least honest one you have — a check-in banner that never turns off, a fabricated sentence about the user's own adherence, and a pulsing Live badge on an engine that does not exist. The concepts below keep the density and delete the fiction.

What we take from whom

  • YAZIOCard-based layout with real breathing room. Reviewers single out the calm as the reason it feels considered rather than busy.
  • LifesumOne summary number that stands for overall quality, plus habit tracking for water. People understand one score faster than four bars.
  • ResearchUsers prefer goals and progress bars over other gamification. Bars beat badges, streaks beat points.
  • MacroFactorShow the trend, not today's scale reading. Daily weight is noise; the smoothed line is the signal.
  • CronometerThe warning: depth without hierarchy reads as utilitarian. Detail must be earned by tapping, never dumped on arrival.

Concept A — the today column

recommended One priority per row, in descending urgency

The idea: the top of the screen answers "am I on track today?" and nothing else. Everything below it is optional. The current layout puts a permanent check-in banner above the macros, so the first thing you see is a demand rather than an answer.

Why this order

  • Question before demand. The macro answer sits above every prompt.
  • The priority row is conditional. It renders only when something is genuinely true — no weigh-in in N days, no plan for today, a save that failed. When nothing is true, the row does not exist.
  • Today's meals are the primary interaction. Ticking one is the single most common daily action and it should be reachable without scrolling.
  • Habits go last and stay visually quiet. They are supporting evidence, not the headline.

Target: 1 action to tick a meal. 1 action to add water.

Concept B — the honest engine card

honesty Three states for a feature that is still being built

The problem: the sidebar says Engine Insights with a pulsing Live badge, and the banner asserts "Adherence was high last week!" — text typed into the source, not measured from anything. The fix is not to hide the ambition. It is to show honest progress toward it.

State 1 — not enough data yet (the truthful default today)
Your targets
Set from your profile. Log weight for 14 days and VioMacro will start adjusting them for you.
3 of 14 weigh-ins · honest progress bar, real count
State 2 — engine active (once it is actually built)
Adjusted +90 kcal this week
Your weight trend fell faster than target. Based on 11 weigh-ins and 6 days of logging.
State 3 — engine needs input
Waiting on this week's weigh-ins
2 more days of data and targets update on Sunday.

Why this is better than hiding it

State 1 tells the user the engine is real and coming, gives them a reason to log, and shows honest progress toward the thing they were promised. That is more motivating than a fake badge — and unlike the fake badge, it survives contact with a sceptical user.

Concept C — the empty dashboard

day one What a brand-new account sees

Currently: zeros in every macro card, a hardcoded 2,000 kcal target if a plan has no targets of its own, and a permanent "Weekly Check-In Required" banner on an account with nothing to check in about. Three separate lies on an empty account.

Welcome, Sara
2,180 kcal · your target
Nothing logged yet today
1. Add a few recipes
Import a photo or PDF, or write one
2. Plan your week
Needs at least one recipe
3. Shop the list
Built from your plan

Three numbered steps, in dependency order, each disabled until the one before it is done. This is the only place in the app where numbering is appropriate — because it genuinely is a sequence, and step 2 really does need step 1.
