Go look at the real ones

These are not in your repo — they belong to the people who made them. But their own store pages carry official screenshots, and flipping through those is worth twenty minutes.

  • MacroFactorApp Store — look at how quiet the logging screen is, and how the trend chart demotes today’s reading.
  • YAZIOApp Store — the polish reference. Watch the spacing and the card edges.
  • LifesumApp Store — how a single summary score replaces four competing numbers.
  • Paprikapaprikaapp.com — recipe ownership done right. Also a lesson in how a dated interface ages.
  • AnyListanylist.com — the grocery list everyone else copies. Note the category ordering.
  • Plan to Eatplantoeat.com — the drag-from-library-into-a-day gesture.
  • Mealimemealime.com — how little you can ask before producing a plan.
  • Cronometercronometer.com — the cautionary one. All the data, none of the hierarchy.

How to use them

Open a few, screenshot anything that makes you stop, and drop the images into docs/design/moodboard/refs/ as your own private reference folder. That stays personal — it just should not ship inside the product or its public docs. Then tell me which details you want and I will draw them properly into this board.
